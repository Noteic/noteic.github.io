Download

Noteic is free in the Microsoft Store: every feature, no trial and no subscription.

Get it free on theMicrosoft Store Open in the Store app

Requirements#

WindowsWindows 11, or Windows 10 version 2004 (build 19041) or later
ProcessorARM64 or x64. The Microsoft Store installs the right version for your PC.
DownloadAbout 150 MB
PenRecommended: the Surface Pen or any other Windows pen. You can also write with the mouse.
AccountNone. Noteic works offline.

Install#

  1. Open Noteic in the Microsoft Store.
  2. Select Get or Install.
  3. Start Noteic from the Start menu.

Next: Getting started.

Updates#

The Microsoft Store keeps Noteic up to date. After a bigger update, Noteic shows once what is new. You can read all changes on What's new.

Uninstall#

Uninstalling Noteic does not delete your notes. They stay as .noteic files in your workspace, by default Documents\Noteic.

Noteic's settings, your lists of recent notes and favorites, and drafts of notes that were never saved are removed. Save your Untitled notes before you uninstall.
