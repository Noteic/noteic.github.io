If Noteic closes unexpectedly

Noteic saves a report on your PC and does not send it anywhere. At the next start it offers Copy report, so you can paste the report into an e-mail or a post on r/Noteic. It may contain names of your notes.

If this happens while Noteic reopens your last tabs, it starts with an empty window and asks you: Reopen tabs or Not now. If the graphics driver failed, Noteic uses compatible graphics on this PC and tries normal graphics again after 7 days.
