Noteic Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

Noteic is a page-based note-taking app for Windows, developed by Stefan Bogdanovic. This policy explains which data Noteic processes and where it goes.

In short

Noteic itself sends no data to the developer. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no telemetry. It goes online only when you ask it to, as described below.

Your notes

Your notebooks are saved as .noteic files in folders you choose on your device. If you choose a folder that a cloud service keeps in sync (for example OneDrive), that service transfers your files under its own privacy policy. Noteic itself does not upload anything.

Data kept on your device

Noteic stores its settings, the list of recently used notebooks, recovery copies of unsaved work and technical logs (error and performance reports) in its app data folders on your device. These files stay on your device unless you pass them on yourself.

Internet access

Noteic connects to the internet only when you ask it to:

  • Images from the web. When you drag an image from a website into a note, or paste a link to an image, Noteic downloads that image directly from the website. The website receives an ordinary web request, like from a browser: it includes your IP address and identifies the program as “Noteic”.
  • Links in notes. When you click a web or e-mail link in a note, Noteic hands it to your default browser or e-mail program.

Noteic sends no other data over the network.

Microsoft Store

Microsoft gives developers statistics and error reports about their apps, such as the number of installs and reports of crashes, according to your Windows diagnostic data settings. This is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The developer uses these reports only to fix errors.

Children

Noteic does not collect data from anyone, including children.
