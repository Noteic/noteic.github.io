Getting started

Noteic opens with an empty page, ready for your pen. Here is how to write, find your tools and keep your notes safe.

Write your first note

  1. Start Noteic from the Start menu. A window opens with a new note called Untitled.
  2. Write on the page with your pen. Touch scrolls and zooms, so your hand can rest on the screen.
  3. No pen? The mouse writes too: hold the left button and draw.

To start another note, open … (More) at the end of the toolbar and choose New document, or press Ctrl+N.

The toolbar

All tools sit in one toolbar. From left to right:

LibraryOpens and closes your notes on the left. Library
PenBallpoint, fountain pen or calligraphy pen. Tap again for its settings. Pens
HighlighterMarks behind your writing. Highlighter
FillFills closed strokes with a see-through color. Fill
EraserErases whole strokes or an area. Eraser
ShapesLines, arrows, rectangles, stars and more. Shapes
Text boxTyped text, tables and formulas. Text
RulerRuler or protractor. Ruler and protractor
Tool drawerMemo tape and laser pointer. Memo tape
Lasso selectionSelect, move, scale and copy. Selection
PanDrag the view with pen, finger or mouse.
PastePastes what you copied.
Widths and colorsFour widths and eight colors for the current pen. Tap the active one again to change it.
Undo, RedoCtrl+Z and Ctrl+Y
PagesShows the page number and opens the page overview. Page overview
…More: new, open, save, images, PDF, full screen and settings.

You can hide tools you never use and move the toolbar to any edge of the window. See Customize the toolbar.

Pen, touch and mouse

  • Pen: writes and works every tool. The eraser end of your pen erases in every tool except Pan.
  • Touch: never writes. One finger scrolls, two fingers pinch to zoom, and a double tap switches between 100% and the page width.
  • Mouse: the left button writes and works every tool. Ctrl and the wheel zoom, Shift and the wheel scroll sideways.

Hold Space and drag to move the view with any tool. Zoom goes from 25% to 500%. When you zoom, a small zoom box appears with presets, Fit page width, Fit page height and Lock zoom.

Where your notes are saved

Your notes are .noteic files. By default Noteic keeps them in Documents\Noteic, your workspace. You can choose another folder, for example in OneDrive, under … › Settings › Choose folder…. Nothing is moved when you switch.

  • Save: … › Save or Ctrl+S. A new note asks for a name and a folder the first time. Save as is Ctrl+Shift+S.
  • Autosave: once a note has a file, Noteic saves it by itself about 20 seconds after your last change. A star (*) in the tab means there are changes not yet in the file.
  • Untitled notes are kept as drafts and come back after a restart.
  • After a restart, Noteic opens your windows and tabs where you left them.
TipThe library is the easiest way to create and find notes: Subjects, Chapters and Notes, like a binder. See Library.
