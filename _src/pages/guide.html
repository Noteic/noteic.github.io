Getting started

Noteic opens with an empty page, ready for your pen. Here is how to write, find your tools and keep your notes safe.

Write your first note

  1. Start Noteic from the Start menu. A window opens with a new note called Untitled.
  2. Write on the page with your pen. Touch scrolls and zooms, so your hand can rest on the screen.
  3. No pen? The mouse writes too: hold the left button and draw.

To start another note, open … (More) at the end of the toolbar and choose New › Page-based document, or press Ctrl+N. For one big page that grows as you write, choose New › Whiteboard. See Whiteboard.

The toolbar

All tools sit in one toolbar. From left to right:

LibraryOpens and closes your notes on the left. Library
PenBallpoint, fountain pen or calligraphy pen. Tap again for its settings. Pens
HighlighterMarks behind your writing. Highlighter
FillFills closed strokes with a see-through color. Fill
EraserErases whole strokes or an area. Eraser
ShapesLines, arrows, rectangles, stars and more. Shapes
Text boxTyped text, tables and formulas. Text
RulerRuler or protractor, in centimeters or inches. Ruler and protractor
Tool drawerMemo tape and laser pointer. Memo tape
Lasso selectionSelect, move, scale, copy and recolor. On a whiteboard also Insert space. Selection
StickersOpens your stickers next to the page. Stickers
PanDrag the view with pen, finger or mouse.
PastePastes what you copied.
Widths and colorsFour widths and eight colors for the current pen. Tap the active one again to change it.
Undo, RedoCtrl+Z and Ctrl+Y
PagesShows the page number and opens the page overview. Page overview. On a whiteboard it says Whiteboard and shows everything at once.
…More: new, open, save, import image, PDF, full screen and settings.

You can hide tools you never use and move the toolbar to any edge of the window. See Customize the toolbar. With a keyboard, P, H, E and L pick the pen, highlighter, eraser and lasso.

Pen, touch and mouse

  • Pen: writes and works every tool. The eraser end of your pen erases in every tool except Pan. The side button can switch to another tool while you hold it, see Pen and mouse buttons.
  • Touch: never writes. One finger scrolls, two fingers pinch to zoom, and a double tap switches between 100% and the page width. On a whiteboard, a double tap switches between 100% and everything on it.
  • Mouse: the left button writes and works every tool. The right button can select like the lasso, see Pen and mouse buttons. Ctrl and the wheel zoom, Shift and the wheel scroll sideways.

Hold Space and drag to move the view with any tool.

Scroll bars

Scroll bars have an arrow at each end. Tap an arrow to scroll one step, or hold it to keep scrolling. You can also grab the handle anywhere across the width of the bar, which is easier with a finger.

Zoom

Zoom goes from 25% to 500%; a whiteboard can zoom out further to show everything on it. When you zoom, a small zoom box appears, with a full screen button on the left. Tap the zoom level in it to open the zoom menu:

  • Zoom levels from 50% to 500%.
  • Fit: Width or Height of the page. On a whiteboard, Show all instead.
  • Lock zoom keeps the zoom where it is.
  • In page-based notes, Lock scrolling holds the view in all directions, Lock sideways only left and right, and Stop at edge stops scrolling at the edge of the pages.

Where your notes are saved

Your notes are .noteic files. By default Noteic keeps them in Documents\Noteic, your workspace. You can choose another folder, for example in OneDrive: open … › Settings and, under Library, tap Use another folder. Your notes stay where they are.

  • Save: … › Save or Ctrl+S. A new note asks for a name and a folder the first time. Save as is Ctrl+Shift+S.
  • Autosave: once a note has a file, Noteic saves it by itself about 20 seconds after your last change. A star (*) in the tab means there are changes not yet in the file.
  • Untitled notes are kept as drafts and come back after a restart.
  • After a restart, Noteic opens your windows and tabs where you left them.
