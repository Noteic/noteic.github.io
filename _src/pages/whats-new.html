What's new

Noteic updates through the Microsoft Store. Here is what each version brought.

Version 1.6 Latest

  • Dark pages: show your pages dark on screen with light ink, while images keep their colors. Files, exports and PDFs stay as they are. Turn it on under … › Settings › Dark pages.
  • Pen side button: hold it to highlight or to use your previous tool, besides lasso and eraser.
  • Right mouse button as lasso: hold it and drag to select. Turn it on under … › Settings › Right mouse button.
  • Keys for tools: P pen, H highlighter, E eraser, L lasso.
  • Insert space uses both sides: when one side reaches the edge of the whiteboard, the other side moves to make room, and Noteic tells you why when it can't.
  • Four new memo tape patterns: flowers, waves, hearts and leaves.
  • Pen cursor: a dot in your pen's color and size, a crosshair or an arrow.
  • Small shapes are easy to move with the lasso, and very small selections get a move button.
  • Arrows on the scroll bars, and a handle you can grab across the whole bar.
  • A full screen button in the zoom box, which stays visible in full screen.
  • Sharper icons in the toolbar and menus.
  • Tidier settings: icons for the side button, a pen drawing for Top button and more room next to the scroll bar.
  • Saving to OneDrive no longer shows “Not responding”, notes that are only online download while you keep working, and big PDFs hold up the window less.
  • If Noteic closes unexpectedly, the next start offers a report you can copy.

Version 1.5.1 30 October 2026

  • Stickers: tap Stickers next to the lasso, then tap a sticker or drag it onto your page or whiteboard.
  • Make your own stickers: select with the lasso and tap Save as sticker, or drag the selection into the Stickers panel.
  • Insert space on the whiteboard: press and hold the lasso button, choose Insert space, then drag down or right to make room.
  • Recolor with your toolbar colors: tap Style in the selection bar and pick a color. Tap it again to change that toolbar color.
  • Pen side button: hold it to select with the lasso or to erase. Choose under … › Settings › Pen side button.
  • Ruler in cm or inches: press and hold the ruler button and choose under Units.
  • Search filters for subject, tag and favorites in the library.
  • Add images to a new note before you save it.
  • Lock scrolling and Stop at edge in the zoom menu.
  • PDF pages show a preview instead of white when you zoom out or scroll slowly.
  • The pen and highlighter menus preview the real stroke.
  • A refined dark mode with a darker toolbar and menus, two new accent colors (indigo and gold), and redesigned Settings and menus.
  • 70 new subject icons, sorted by field of study.

Version 1.5 24 October 2026

  • Whiteboard: one page that grows on all four sides as you write, up to about 10.3 × 21.2 m. Tap + in the library and choose Whiteboard, or open … › New › Whiteboard.
  • Show all, or double-tap with your finger, to see everything on your whiteboard.
  • Export what you see: the visible area as PDF on A4 or A3, or the whole whiteboard in sheets. Choose under Save as type.
  • 14 new shapes in a new shapes menu, including 3D shapes, block arrows and a number line.
  • Color fields you can change for shapes, pens and highlighters.
  • A tidier library: create and rename (F2) right in the list, and color subjects, chapters and tags with 16 colors you can change.
  • Swiping stays straight, and a clearer zoom menu with Lock sideways.
  • Extend page borders up to 20 cm left and right, and pages stay centered under each other.
  • Drag a tab onto another window, or use … › Move to other window.
  • A back button in menus and bigger toolbar icons.
  • Faster saving, less memory for big PDFs and fixes for rare crashes.

Version 1.4 14 October 2026

  • Handwriting to text: select your ink with the lasso and tap Convert to text. Noteic uses the Windows handwriting recognition for your language, on your device.
  • Customize your toolbar: keep the tools you use, move the rest into the drawer or hide them.
  • New paper styles: graph, music, writing and hexagon paper, with line weight, your own colors and a page menu with live previews.
  • A calmer gray light mode, clearer tabs and easier-to-read dialogs.
  • Petrol as a new accent color, and menus always stay inside the Noteic window.

Version 1.3 8 October 2026

  • Extend a page on all four sides for more room, also around PDF pages with a paper pattern
  • Tables for typed text: rows, columns, merged cells and cell colors
  • A new text toolbar with links, line spacing, more fonts and a sixth highlight color
  • Text boxes: move them by the strip on top and rotate them with the knob below
  • Text pasted from websites, Word and OneNote keeps its formatting
  • Memo tape (formerly Study tape): washi ends and three new patterns
  • Tidier menus in blocks that scroll with pen, finger and mouse and never cover the taskbar
  • Page overview: 15 brighter bookmark colors and a clearer page menu
  • Choose the toolbar position on a simple layout
  • Rounder shapes and all memo tape patterns in the PDF export
  • A PDF page keeps its PDF when you pick a paper pattern

Version 1.2 5 October 2026

  • Study tape: cover answers and tap to reveal them
  • Links on your handwriting to a spot in your notes
  • Drag a selection onto another page
  • Smoother writing with mouse and tablet
  • Smoother scrolling and a smoother library
  • The folder path of the note in the window title
  • Closing a window moves its tabs to another window
  • A warmer light mode and a pink laser pointer
  • Less memory and CPU use
  • “About Noteic” in the “…” menu

Version 1.1 2 October 2026

  • Noteic is now free: every feature, no trial, no subscription
  • Workspaces: switch between note folders, for example one per semester
  • Sub-notes, like subpages
  • Library: your own order with drag handles for pen, finger and mouse; a tap on a note selects it, its menu opens it; tags on their own line; 24 folder colors plus custom colors; 70 subject icons
  • Fill: one tap on the fill button turns it on or off; the 20 fill colors can be changed and are saved
  • On x64 devices with external pen tablets, strokes no longer look thicker while you write
  • Slightly larger toolbar

Version 1.0 29 September 2026

First release in the Microsoft Store.
