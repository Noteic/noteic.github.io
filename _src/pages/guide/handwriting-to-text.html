Handwriting to text New in 1.4

Select your handwriting and tap once: Noteic turns it into a text box you can edit, right where you wrote.

Before you start

Noteic uses the handwriting recognition built into Windows. It runs on your device and works offline. Add handwriting for your language once:

  1. Open Windows Settings › Time & language › Language & region.
  2. Next to your language, select … › Language options.
  3. Under Handwriting, install handwriting if Windows offers a download.

If no recognition is installed, Noteic says so: “No handwriting recognition — see Windows Settings › Time & language”.

Convert

  1. Select your handwriting with the lasso.
  2. Tap Convert to text in the selection bar.
  • Your ink is replaced by one text box in the same place, with one line per line you wrote.
  • The text gets your default text box font, a size that matches your writing, and your ink color. Words in another color keep their color.
  • Slanted writing is recognized too.
  • Changed your mind? Ctrl+Z brings your handwriting back.

Correct a word

Right after converting, while the new text box is still selected, tap a word. A small bar shows your original ink for that word, up to four suggestions and Edit text.
