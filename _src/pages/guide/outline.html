Outline New in 1.7

A table of contents for your note, with up to four levels. One tap on an entry takes you to its page. Imported PDFs bring their bookmarks, and Export as PDF turns your outline into bookmarks.

Open the outline

  1. Tap the page number in the toolbar, or press Ctrl+G. Pages opens.
  2. At the top, choose Outline, next to All and Bookmarked.

The outline is a list on the left, with your pages in two columns next to it. Each entry shows its page number on the right, and the entry your current page belongs to is marked.

  • Noteic remembers the tab you chose. The next time, Pages opens with it again.
  • In a narrow window, for example with two apps side by side, the Outline tab shows only the list. In a very narrow one, Bookmarked and Outline show as icons.
  • The outline belongs to page-based notes. A whiteboard has none.

The other two tabs are described in Pages and paper › Page overview.

Add an entry

  1. Go to the page the entry should lead to.
  2. Open Pages › Outline and tap Add current page below the list. In a note without entries, the button is in the middle, under “No outline yet”.
  3. Type a title. Noteic suggests one from the top of the page: the first line of a PDF's text, of a text box or of your handwriting. If it finds nothing, the suggestion is the page, for example “Page 6”.
  4. Choose a Level from 1 to 4 and tap Add, or press Enter.

While you type, a dashed row in the list shows where the new entry will be. Entries always follow the order of your pages, and a page can have more than one entry.

  • Any other page: in Pages, open the ⋮ menu under a page and choose Add to outline…. This works in all three tabs.
  • Levels: level 1 is the top level, levels 2 to 4 are sub-entries. An entry can be at most one level deeper than the entry above it, so the first entry is always level 1. Levels you can't choose are grayed out.
  • Titles can have up to 200 characters. The list shortens a long title with “…”. Rest the pointer on it to read all of it.

Jump to a page

  • Tap an entry: Noteic goes to its page and closes Pages.
  • Fold and unfold: tap the small arrow in front of an entry to hide or show its sub-entries. Noteic keeps this until you close the tab of the note.
  • Scroll the pages next to the list, and the mark in the outline follows.

Rename, change the level, delete

The marked entry shows a … button, and so does the entry under your mouse pointer. Tap it, right-click an entry, or press and hold an entry. The menu has:

RenameType the new title and tap Rename, or press Enter.
IndentOne level deeper. Sub-entries move with their entry.
OutdentOne level higher. Sub-entries move with their entry.
Delete entryRemoves the entry; the page stays. Its sub-entries move up one level. At the bottom of Pages, “Entry deleted” appears with Undo.

What is not possible is grayed out, for example Indent on level 4.

  • Drag to change the level: drag an entry to the right or to the left, with pen, finger or mouse. A label shows the new level, for example “Level 3”, or “Level 2 · 3 entries” when sub-entries move along. “Max. 4 levels” means the entry or one of its sub-entries would go deeper than level 4.
  • Move an entry: entries follow their pages. Drag the page to another place in Pages, and its entry moves with it.
  • Delete a page, and its entries go with it. A page you duplicate or copy gets no entry.
  • Undo: every change to the outline is one step. Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y also work while Pages is open.

PDF bookmarks

Many PDFs come with their own table of contents, called bookmarks. Noteic keeps them on the way in and on the way out.

  • Import: when you import a PDF, the bookmarks of the pages you import become outline entries. Entries you already have stay. Bookmarks that lead to a website or to another file are left out. Undo takes back the pages and their entries in one step.
  • Levels: bookmarks down to level 4 keep their level, deeper ones become level 4. Bookmarks under a bookmark that is left out move up. One import brings up to 1,000 entries; if a PDF has more, the deepest levels are left out first.
  • Long outlines: an outline with more than 300 entries opens folded, with the branch of your current page open.
  • Export: Export as PDF writes your outline into the PDF as bookmarks, with the same levels as in the list. If you export only some pages, only their entries go along, and sub-entries of an entry that stays behind move up.
Outline or bookmarks?The colored bookmarks you set on pages in Pages are marks for yourself: they stay in Noteic and are not exported. What a PDF calls bookmarks is the outline.
