Tabs and windows

Keep several notes open in tabs, or put two notes side by side in their own windows.

Tabs

  • Every note you open gets a tab. Close it with its ×, a middle click, or Ctrl+W.
  • Drag tabs to reorder them.
  • Ctrl+Tab goes to the tab you used last, Ctrl+Page Down and Ctrl+Page Up to the next and previous tab.
  • With many tabs, the tab bar scrolls and an arrow lists all tabs.

Noteic keeps up to 12 notes open. When you open more, it saves and closes the tab you used longest ago. Untitled notes are never closed this way.

Windows

  • … › New window moves the current tab into its own window.
  • Or drag a tab down out of the tab bar: it opens in a new window where you drop it.
  • To move a tab into another Noteic window, drag it onto the tab bar of that window. A mark shows where it lands. Or use … › Move to other window.
  • When you close a window while another is open, its tabs move to the other window.

The window title shows where the note lives, for example “Noteic › Statistics › Week 2 › Lecture”.

Full screen

… › Full screen, F11 or the full screen button in the zoom box hides the title bar and scroll bars; tabs and toolbar stay. The zoom box appears when you zoom.

In full screen, the zoom box stays visible. Tap the button in it or press F11 again to leave.
