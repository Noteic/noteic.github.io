Links

Jump from a word to the page where you explain it, or from typed text to a website.

Link handwriting to a spot

  1. Select the handwriting or shape with the lasso.
  2. Tap Anchor link in the selection bar, or press Ctrl+K.
  3. Go to the target, on any page of the note, and press and hold there.

Linked ink gets a dashed blue underline with a small chain. Tap Anchor link on it again for Go to anchor, Move anchor and Remove link.

Link text

  1. In a text box, select a word and tap Link in the text toolbar, or press Ctrl+K.
  2. Type a web address or an e-mail address and tap Insert link.
  3. Or tap Link to a spot and tap the target in your notes.

Text links are blue and underlined. To change one, put the cursor in it and press Ctrl+K.

Follow a link

  • Tap linked ink with your finger, or point at it with the lasso: a small bar shows the target and Open link.
  • Web and e-mail links open in your browser or e-mail program. Links to a spot scroll right to it.
