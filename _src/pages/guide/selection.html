Selection and lasso

Circle what you want to change. Then move it, scale it, rotate it, copy it, give it a new color or drag it onto another page.

Select

  1. Tap Lasso selection in the toolbar.
  2. Draw a loop around what you want. Everything completely inside the loop is selected.
  3. Tap the button again to go back to the pen.
  • Rectangle instead of lasso: press and hold the button and choose Rectangle.
  • Tap an item to select just that one. Tap empty paper to clear the selection.
  • Add more: hold Shift while you draw or tap.
  • Everything on the page: Ctrl+A.

You can select handwriting, highlighter, shapes, text boxes, images, formulas, tables and memo tape. A selection always stays on one page.

Move, scale and rotate

  • Move: drag inside the frame. Release it over another page to move it there.
  • Scale: drag a corner. Line widths scale along.
  • Stretch: drag the middle of a side.
  • Rotate: drag the round knob above the frame. Hold Shift for 15° steps.

A single line, arrow or polygon also shows dots on its points. Drag them to reshape it; dragging the middle of a curved arrow bends it.

The selection bar

Under the selection, a small bar offers what fits your selection:

  • Convert to text for handwriting. See Handwriting to text.
  • Edit text box, Edit formula or Crop image when one of those is selected.
  • Cut, Copy and Duplicate.
  • Style: line width, line style, fill and color for ink and shapes.
  • Anchor link: link the selection to a spot in your notes. See Links.
  • Delete.

Copy and paste

  • Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X and Ctrl+V work as usual. Ctrl+D duplicates.
  • Paste lands under the pointer, or in the middle of the view.
  • Ctrl+Shift+V, or pressing and holding the Paste button, pastes at the same position as the original. Handy for copying to another page.
  • When you copy, Noteic also puts a picture of the selection on the Windows clipboard, so you can paste it into other apps.

Delete removes the selection, Esc clears it.
