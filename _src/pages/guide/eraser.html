Eraser

Erase whole strokes or a whole area, choose what the eraser removes, or simply flip your pen.

Erase

  1. Tap Eraser in the toolbar. Its menu opens.
  2. Choose a mode:
    • Stroke: removes every stroke you touch, as a whole. It also removes shapes when you touch their outline, and memo tape. Set the size from 0.5 to 20 mm.
    • Area: drag a rectangle. When you lift the pen, everything it touches is removed, including text boxes and images.
  3. Erase on the page. One erase can be undone with Ctrl+Z.

What the eraser removes

Under Erases:

  • Everything (the default)
  • Highlighter: only highlighter, your writing stays as it is.
  • Tape: only memo tape, your writing stays as it is.

Writing under covering memo tape is protected: the eraser hits the tape, not what is underneath.

More options

  • Deselect automatically (on by default): after erasing once, Noteic goes back to the tool you used before.
  • Clear all erases everything on the current page. You can undo it.

Erase with your pen

Turn your pen around: its eraser end, or an eraser button that Windows reports as one, erases in every tool except Pan, with the mode you set. Your current tool stays selected.
