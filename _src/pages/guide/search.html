Search your notes New in 1.7

Find what you wrote by hand, and typed text, tables, formulas and PDF text. Press Ctrl+F in a note, or search inside all your notes from the library. Your handwriting is read on your PC, offline.

Search in a note

  1. Press Ctrl+F. A search field opens at the top right of the page: Find in this note.
  2. Type at least two characters. Noteic searches while you type and goes to the first exact result from the page you are on.
  3. Press Enter or F3 for the next result, Shift+Enter or Shift+F3 for the previous one. The two arrows in the search field do the same.
  4. Press Esc or tap the × in the search field to close the search.
  • If you selected text in a text box or in a PDF first, Ctrl+F starts with that text.
  • You can keep writing while the search is open. F3 works from the page too; Enter only while the cursor is in the search field.
  • From the page, Esc first clears a selection and then closes the search.
  • The search belongs to the note you see. Switching to another tab closes it.
  • Ctrl+F does nothing while Pages, Settings or the formula editor is open.
No keyboard?Search from the library: a tap on a note under Inside notes opens it with the search field. With your pen you can write in the search field.

What search finds

  • Handwriting: the words Noteic has read from your ink. Highlighter strokes are not read.
  • Typed text in text boxes.
  • Tables: the text in their cells.
  • Formulas: by their LaTeX text.
  • PDF text: the text of imported PDF pages.

How Noteic compares your words:

  • Upper and lower case and accents make no difference. ß also matches ss, and ä, ö, ü also match ae, oe, ue.
  • A word is found by its beginning. From four characters on, it is also found inside a longer word.
  • Several words are found where they follow one another.

Results on the page

  • Every result is marked in yellow. The current one is stronger and has a frame in your accent color.
  • The counter shows where you are, for example “2 of 8 · 1 similar”. In a narrow window it shortens to “2/8”.
  • Similar results: handwriting is not always read exactly. Where only another reading of your handwriting matches, or a word is just close to yours, the result counts as similar. Similar results are marked more faintly and come after the exact ones.
  • Noteic moves the view only when a result is not fully visible, and keeps your zoom.
  • “No results” appears when nothing was found in what Noteic could read. While pages are still waiting to be read, the line below the search field says “No results yet” instead.

While Noteic is still reading

Handwriting and PDF text have to be read before they can be found. In a long note this takes a moment. A line below the search field shows how far Noteic is, and results appear as pages are read:

“Reading handwriting… 12 of 40 pages”Noteic is reading the handwriting of this note.
“Reading PDF text… 120 of 500 pages”Noteic is reading the text of the PDF pages.
“12 of 40 pages read”, “No results yet · 28 pages not read” or “Not all pages read”Reading stopped before the end, for example because you were writing. Tap Continue.
“5 scanned pages have no searchable text”These PDF pages contain no text, as scans do, so there is no PDF text to find on them. What you wrote on them is still found.
“Handwriting can't be searched on this PC”Noteic found no handwriting recognition in Windows. Tap Open Windows settings and see Before you start.
“Handwriting reading is paused”Read handwriting for search is turned off, or reading failed several times. Tap Settings.

Search inside all your notes

  1. Open the library (Ctrl+B) and tap Search, the magnifier on the left.
  2. Type at least two characters. Under Results you see the Notes and Chapters whose name matches. Below them, Inside notes lists the notes that contain your text.
  3. Tap a note under Inside notes. Noteic opens it, opens the search field with your text and goes to the first result.
  • Each line shows what was found and on which pages, for example “3 hits · 1 similar · p. 12, 14, 31”.
  • The filters below the search field (All or Favorites, one Subject, one tag) apply here too.
  • Inside notes shows up to 24 notes. If more match, type more precisely.
  • If you were writing while Noteic searched, you may see “Not all notes searched”. Tap Continue.

Notes that were not read yet

Inside notes looks through what Noteic has already read from your notes: handwriting, typed text, tables and formulas. If some notes were not read yet, a line below the list says so, for example “2 notes not read yet”.

  1. Tap Read now.
  2. Wait for “Reading handwriting… 3 of 20 notes” to finish. Stay in Search: reading stops when you leave it.
  3. The list is searched again by itself. If notes are left, tap Read now again.
  • Read now reads up to 20 notes at a time. On battery it also stops after 100 pages.
  • Notes that are open in a tab are not counted here. An open note is read when you search in it with Ctrl+F, or in the background while your PC is plugged in.
  • PDF text is not read here. The library finds PDF text in a note after you searched that note once with Ctrl+F.
  • Notes in a cloud folder that are only online, not on this PC, are not read.

How your handwriting is read

  • On your PC, offline. Noteic uses the handwriting recognition built into Windows, the same as Convert to text. Your ink is not sent anywhere for it.
  • In the background. Reading runs in a second Noteic process and waits while you write.
  • When: while your PC is plugged in, battery saver is off and Noteic is the active app, Noteic reads your open and recently opened notes in the background. Otherwise it reads when you search in a note or tap Read now.
  • Your language: Windows needs handwriting recognition for the language you write in. See Before you start.
  • What is kept: the words Noteic reads are kept on this PC and, when you save, in the note. See what Noteic stores.

Turn reading off

  1. Open … (More) at the end of the toolbar and tap Settings, or press Ctrl+,.
  2. Choose Library. See Settings › Library.
  3. Turn off Read handwriting for search. It is on from the start.

Noteic then reads no more handwriting. Typed text, tables, formulas and PDF text stay searchable, and so does handwriting that was read before. Where handwriting was not read yet, the search shows “Handwriting reading is paused” with Settings. In the library, this line takes the place of Read now. When you turn the switch on again, an open search in a note continues reading by itself.

Clear recognized words

Below the switch, Clear recognized words removes the words Noteic has read from your handwriting on this PC. Noteic asks first; confirm with Clear. Your notes stay as they are, and Noteic reads your handwriting again when you search.

Write in the search field with your pen

Hold your pen over the search field of a note or of the library, or tap it with the pen. A handwriting field with the hint Write here opens over it. Write your word and tap Insert text: it goes into the search field as typed text. Strike through a word or scribble over it to delete it. All details are in Write with your pen.

Limits

  • Search needs at least two letters or digits.
  • Text in images and on scanned PDF pages is not found.
  • Handwriting is found once it was read, and only as well as Windows could read it. If a word is not found, try its first letters or look at the similar results.
  • Without handwriting recognition in Windows, Noteic can't search handwriting. Typed text, tables, formulas and PDF text are still found.
  • In the library, Inside notes only knows notes that were read before, and PDF text only from notes you searched with Ctrl+F.
