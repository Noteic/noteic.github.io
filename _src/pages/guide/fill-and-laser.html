Fill and laser pointer

Fill gives closed strokes an inner color. The laser pointer shows where you point, without ever touching your note.

Fill

The Fill button (the bucket) is a switch for the pen and the highlighter:

  1. Tap Fill to turn it on. Its menu opens.
  2. Pick an Inner color from 20 colors and set the Opacity from 5% to 100%.
  3. Draw a closed stroke, for example a circle around a word. The inside fills with the color; the outline keeps the pen's own color.
  4. Tap Fill again to turn it off.

Tap the selected color again to change it. Your fill colors are saved. Strokes drawn along the ruler are not filled.

To fill shapes, use the Fill switch in the shapes menu instead.

Laser pointer

The laser pointer is in the tool drawer by default. You can move it into the toolbar in Customize the toolbar.

  1. Open the tool drawer and tap Laser pointer. A check mark shows it is on.
  2. Point and draw with pen, finger or mouse. The trail stays for about two seconds and then fades.
  3. Choose another tool to turn it off.

While the laser is on, the color buttons offer five laser colors: red, green, blue, yellow and pink. The trail never becomes part of your note.
