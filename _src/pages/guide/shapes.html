Shapes

45 shapes, from lines and arrows to 3D shapes and speech bubbles. Or sketch by hand and let Noteic recognize the shape.

Draw a shape

  1. Tap Shapes in the toolbar. The shapes menu opens.
  2. Pick a shape, a line width and a line style.
  3. Drag on the page from one corner to the other.

Hold Shift while you drag: lines and arrows snap to 45°, a rectangle becomes a square, an ellipse a circle, and every other shape gets equal width and height.

All shapes

  • Lines: line, arrow, double arrow, curved arrow, curved double arrow, each arrow also with solid heads, angle and axes.
  • Brackets: curly, round and square, as one bracket or as a pair around the area you drag.
  • Number line with a tick every 5 mm.
  • Areas: rectangle, rounded rectangle, capsule, ellipse, triangle, rounded triangle, right triangle, diamond, parallelogram, isosceles trapezoid, pentagon, hexagon, heptagon, octagon, star and heart.
  • Block arrows: a block arrow that points left or right as you drag, and a block double arrow.
  • Callouts: square and rounded speech bubble, and a wavy frame.
  • 3D shapes: cube, cylinder, sphere, cone, pyramid and prism, with the hidden edges dashed.

The solid-head arrows, the number line, the heptagon, the block arrows and the 3D shapes came with Noteic 1.5.

Drawing modes

The top row of the menu changes how you draw:

  • Recognize: draw freely. When you lift the pen, a line, circle, ellipse, triangle, rectangle or polygon becomes a clean shape. Draw from one shape's outline to another's and you get a clean connector. If nothing is recognized, your stroke stays as ink.
  • Polygon: tap corner by corner; a tap on the first corner closes it. Esc discards it.
  • Grid snapping: straight lines whose ends snap to the paper grid and to the corners of other shapes, at 0°, 45° or 90°.
  • Spline: tap to set points, drag while setting a point to shape the curve. Tap the first point to close it, or release Esc to finish.

Tap any shape in the list to go back to plain dragging.

Line, fill and color

  • Line: width from 0.1 to 2 mm, or one tap on Thin lines (0.25 mm), Normal lines (0.5 mm) or Bold lines (1 mm).
  • Seven line styles: Solid, Dashed, Long dashes, Dotted, Dash-dot, Wavy and Zigzag.
  • Fill: fills closed shapes. Switch between Outline and Fill to give each its own color, and set the fill opacity from 5% to 100%.
  • 20 color fields you can change: tap a field to use it, tap it again to change it. One more field at the end adds your own color.
  • The palette button opens a custom color picker with a Hex box.

Change a shape later

Select it with the lasso. Drag the frame to move, scale or rotate it, drag its point dots to reshape it, and use Style in the selection bar to change width, line style, fill and color. See Style and color.
