Keyboard shortcuts

Noteic is made for the pen, but these keys save time when a keyboard is attached.

Files

Ctrl+NNew page-based document
Ctrl+OOpen
Ctrl+SSave
Ctrl+Shift+SSave as

Tools

PThe pen you used last
HHighlighter
EEraser, without opening its menu
LSelection, in the shape its button shows: lasso, rectangle or Select text, on a whiteboard lasso, rectangle or Insert space

Press these keys alone. They do nothing while you type in a text box or a search field. See Keys for your tools.

Editing

Ctrl+ZUndo
Ctrl+YRedo
Ctrl+X, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+VCut, copy, paste. With text selected in a PDF, Ctrl+C copies that text
Ctrl+Shift+VPaste at the same position as the original
Ctrl+DDuplicate the selection
Ctrl+ASelect everything on the page
Shift+H, Shift+VFlip the selection horizontally or vertically
DeleteDelete the selection
EscClear the selection or the text selected in a PDF, leave the Pan tool, cancel a polygon
Ctrl+KAnchor link for the selection, or a link in text
ShiftWhile drawing shapes: 45°, squares and circles. While rotating: 15° steps

Search New in 1.7

Ctrl+FSearch in the note. Pressed again, it selects the text in the search field
Enter, Shift+EnterNext and previous result, while the cursor is in the search field
F3, Shift+F3Next and previous result, also from the page
EscClose the search. From the page, Esc clears a selection first

See Search your notes.

View and pages

Ctrl++, Ctrl+-Zoom in, zoom out
Ctrl+0Zoom to 100%
Space + dragMove the view with any tool
Page Up, Page DownPrevious and next page
Ctrl+Home, Ctrl+EndFirst and last page
Ctrl+GOpen or close Pages: the page overview, with Bookmarked and Outline. Not on a whiteboard
Ctrl+LShow or hide the ruler or protractor
Ctrl+BLibrary (in a text box: bold)
F11Full screen

Zoom window New in 1.8

EscClose the zoom window. An open menu of the strip closes first
Mouse wheelOver the strip: move the frame line by line
Shift + mouse wheelOver the strip: move the frame along the line
Ctrl + mouse wheelOver the strip: change the zoom

See Zoom window.

Outline New in 1.7

In Pages › Outline:

↑, ↓, Home, EndPrevious, next, first and last entry
→, ←Unfold or fold an entry with sub-entries
EnterGo to the page of the entry
F2Rename
Alt+Shift+→, Alt+Shift+←Indent and Outdent: one level deeper or higher
DeleteDelete entry. The page stays
Shift+F10 or the menu keyOpen the menu of the entry

See Outline › Keys in the outline.

Library

F2Rename the Note, Chapter, Subject or tag you tapped last
EnterConfirm a new name
EscCancel a new name

Tabs

Ctrl+TabTab you used last
Ctrl+Shift+TabNext tab in the row
Ctrl+Page Down, Ctrl+Page UpNext and previous tab
Ctrl+WClose the tab

Text boxes

Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, Ctrl+UBold, italic, underline
Tab, Shift+TabIndent lists; in tables, next and previous cell
EscClose the text box. If the handwriting field is open, Esc closes that first
Ctrl+EnterInsert the formula (in the formula editor)

Settings and menus New in 1.7

Ctrl+,Open or close Settings
EscIn Settings: one step back, then close
Backspace or Alt+←In Settings: one step back
↑, ↓, Home, EndIn Settings: move through the categories
Tab, arrow keys, EscIn the open shapes menu: Tab moves into the menu and from part to part, the arrow keys move within a part, Esc closes the menu

See Settings and Shapes › The shapes menu.
