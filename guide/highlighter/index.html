Highlighter

The highlighter marks behind your writing, so your ink and text always stay readable on top.

Use the highlighter#

  1. Tap Highlighter in the toolbar.
  2. Pick a width and a color. The highlighter has its own colors, starting with yellow, lime, pink, sky blue, orange and purple.
  3. Draw over your notes. The marks sit behind pen ink, shapes and text.

Highlighter ink is see-through. Widths go up to 10 mm.

Settings#

Tap the active highlighter again to open its settings:

  • Pressure sensitive: on by default.
  • Hold to straighten: hold the pen still at the end of a stroke and it becomes a straight line.
  • Recognize shapes: draw a line, rectangle, triangle, ellipse or circle, and when you lift the pen it becomes a clean shape, still in highlighter.
  • Line ends Round or Square. Square, like a real marker, is the default.
TipDraw along the ruler for perfectly straight highlights. To remove only highlights, set the eraser to Highlighter. See Eraser.
