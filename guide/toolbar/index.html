Customize the toolbar New in 1.4

Keep only the tools you use. Move the rest into the drawer or hide them, and put the toolbar where it suits you.

Choose your tools#

  1. Open … › Settings › Customize toolbar….
  2. For each tool, choose Toolbar, Drawer or Off.
  3. Set how many Widths (up to 4) and Colors (up to 10) the toolbar shows.
  • You can set Library, Highlighter, Fill, Eraser, Shapes, Text box, Ruler, Lasso selection, Pan, Paste, Memo tape and Laser pointer.
  • Library and Pan can go into the drawer but not off. The pen, Undo and Redo always stay.
  • Memo tape and the laser pointer start in the drawer.
  • Reset brings back the standard toolbar.

The tool drawer#

Tools set to Drawer wait behind the drawer button in the toolbar. Tap it to open the drawer and pick a tool. The drawer button only shows when something is in it.

Move the toolbar#

  • Drag the grip at the start of the toolbar to the top, bottom, left or right edge of the window.
  • Or tap an edge in the small window picture under … › Settings.
