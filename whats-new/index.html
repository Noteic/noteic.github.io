What's new

Noteic updates through the Microsoft Store. Here is what each version brought.

Coming soon: WhiteboardAn infinite canvas is on its way to Noteic. Watch the preview or follow r/Noteic to hear first.

Version 1.4 14 October 2026#

  • Handwriting to text: select your ink with the lasso and tap Convert to text. Noteic uses the Windows handwriting recognition for your language, on your device.
  • Customize your toolbar: keep the tools you use, move the rest into the drawer or hide them.
  • New paper styles: graph, music, writing and hexagon paper, with line weight, your own colors and a page menu with live previews.
  • A calmer gray light mode, clearer tabs and easier-to-read dialogs.
  • Petrol as a new accent color, and menus always stay inside the Noteic window.

Version 1.3 8 October 2026#

  • Extend a page on all four sides for more room, also around PDF pages with a paper pattern
  • Tables for typed text: rows, columns, merged cells and cell colors
  • A new text toolbar with links, line spacing, more fonts and a sixth highlight color
  • Text boxes: move them by the strip on top and rotate them with the knob below
  • Text pasted from websites, Word and OneNote keeps its formatting
  • Memo tape (formerly Study tape): washi ends and three new patterns
  • Tidier menus in blocks that scroll with pen, finger and mouse and never cover the taskbar
  • Page overview: 15 brighter bookmark colors and a clearer page menu
  • Choose the toolbar position on a simple layout
  • Rounder shapes and all memo tape patterns in the PDF export
  • A PDF page keeps its PDF when you pick a paper pattern

Version 1.2 5 October 2026#

  • Study tape: cover answers and tap to reveal them
  • Links on your handwriting to a spot in your notes
  • Drag a selection onto another page
  • Smoother writing with mouse and tablet
  • Smoother scrolling and a smoother library
  • The folder path of the note in the window title
  • Closing a window moves its tabs to another window
  • A warmer light mode and a pink laser pointer
  • Less memory and CPU use
  • “About Noteic” in the “…” menu

Version 1.1 2 October 2026#

  • Noteic is now free: every feature, no trial, no subscription
  • Workspaces: switch between note folders, for example one per semester
  • Sub-notes, like subpages
  • Library: your own order with drag handles for pen, finger and mouse; a tap on a note selects it, its menu opens it; tags on their own line; 24 folder colors plus custom colors; 70 subject icons
  • Fill: one tap on the fill button turns it on or off; the 20 fill colors can be changed and are saved
  • On x64 devices with external pen tablets, strokes no longer look thicker while you write
  • Slightly larger toolbar

Version 1.0 29 September 2026#

First release in the Microsoft Store.
